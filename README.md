Fidel Enterprise - Mindset Training Website
Overview
Fidel Enterprise is a mindset training company dedicated to empowering individuals through personal growth and development. This website serves as a platform to showcase the company’s programs, services, and success stories while providing an engaging user experience.

Features
Homepage: Overview of Fidel Enterprise and its mission
Programs & Services: Detailed descriptions of training programs
Testimonials: Success stories from clients
Blog Section: Articles on personal development and mindset growth
Contact Page: Easy access to reach out for inquiries
Tech Stack
React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, NodeJs
